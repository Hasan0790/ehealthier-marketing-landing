What are PROMs, PREMs and DROMs, and why are they important?

Patient-Reported Outcome Measures (PROMs):
Standardised and validated questionnaires used to capture patients' direct feedback on their health status, symptoms, and functional wellbeing. PROMs provide insight into how a patient perceives their own health and quality of life, helping healthcare providers tailor treatments to individual needs.

Patient Experience Measures (PREMs):
PREMs are short questionnaires that collect direct feedback from the patients throughout their health care journey. It reflects their experiences with healthcare services, providing insights into satisfaction and the quality of care. PREMs cover aspects like communication, accessibility, and overall care quality, highlighting areas where services can be improved.

Device-Reported Outcome Measures (DROMs):
Data collected through digital devices such as wearables, mobile apps, and remote monitoring tools that track various health metrics, including activity levels, heart rate, and sleep patterns. DROMs offer objective insights that complement PROMs and PREMs for a more comprehensive view of patient health.
